### Description

The ICAI Lab program is a research collaboration. In the AI for Energy Grids Lab, multiple PhD students are working on projects related to energy grids and net congestion. A challenge is to share the knowledge gained in these projects and make it available for the outside world. This is where the HAN comes in. On the one hand our students from Communication and Multimedia Design work together with the PhD students to make their complicated research more accessible to a general public in creative ways. On the other hand we work on a knowledge platform based on Obsidian to support researchers to share their early work and insights earlier than when a paper is published so that other researchers kan benefit.
